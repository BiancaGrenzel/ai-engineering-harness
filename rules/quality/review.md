# Review

Use this checklist before considering work complete.

## Checklist

- **Correctness** — Does the change do what was requested and nothing false?
- **Scope** — Is the change limited to the objective?
- **Maintainability** — Will this be clear and safe to change later?
- **Edge cases** — Are failure modes and boundary conditions handled?
- **Security** — Are permissions, secrets, and external trust handled appropriately?
- **Tests** — Was verification proportional to risk, and were results reported honestly?
- **Documentation** — Were non-obvious decisions, configs, or contracts updated when needed?

## Review posture

Prefer finding real defects over restating style preferences.

If an issue is out of scope, note it without expanding the change unless required for correctness.
